voyager3 is a fast-paced 3D arena shooter built in Godot 4.6 for three-player
LAN matches. Players fight through six last-one-standing rounds using movement,
map knowledge, ray guns, and collectible equipment that changes health,
mobility, damage, and fire rate.

The arena includes jump pads and speed strips, while eliminated players can
spectate in first person or free-fly around the level. The game was submitted
to the 2026 MAC Game Jam and is available as a Windows download.
