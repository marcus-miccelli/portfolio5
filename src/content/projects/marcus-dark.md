Marcus Dark is designed for long sessions: near-black surfaces, low glare,
readable text, and saturated syntax colours drawn from IBM's Carbon palette.

The workbench theme gives hovered, selected, focused, and active elements their
own restrained surface levels. A companion icon theme adapts Seti-derived file
glyphs for greater clarity against the darker Explorer.

The extension is tuned around IBM Plex Mono, packaged for VS Code, and
available from the Visual Studio Marketplace under the MIT license.
