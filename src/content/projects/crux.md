Crux lets climbers photograph a wall, identify a route, and share attempts,
beta, and sends with other people at their gym. Its computer-vision pipeline
detects holds in a wall image, groups them by colour, and connects the result to
a location-aware social feed.

The hackathon project combines Next.js, Supabase, Roboflow, Leaflet, and
OpenStreetMap. Marcus acted as project lead, led UX work, contributed frontend
features, and worked across the full stack.
